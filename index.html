What Is rbc.com/activate?

rbc.com/activate is the official Royal Bank of Canada web portal for activating new and replacement credit cards issued by RBC.

When you receive a new RBC credit card in the mail — whether it's a new card, a renewal, or a replacement for a lost or stolen card — it arrives inactive for security reasons. Before you can make any purchases, you must complete the activation process through one of three official RBC channels: the online portal at rbc.com/activate, the RBC Mobile app, or by phone.

RBC updated its activation portal in 2026 to support faster biometric identity verification, making it easier than ever to get your card ready in minutes.

⚠️ Security Warning: Always type rbc.com/activate directly into your browser. Never click activation links from emails or text messages you did not request — these are common phishing scams. RBC will never ask for your full card number via email.

Before You Begin — What You Need

Gather the following before visiting rbc.com/activate to ensure a smooth activation:

  • Your new RBC credit card (physical card in hand)
  • 16-digit card number printed on the front
  • 3-digit CVV security code on the back of the card
  • Card expiry date (MM/YY on the front)
  • RBC Online Banking username and password (or SIN to enroll for the first time)
  • Your registered Canadian mailing address
  • A secure internet connection and up-to-date browser

How to Activate Your RBC Card at rbc.com/activate

Follow these five steps to activate your card online. The entire process takes under 5 minutes.

  1. 1

    Visit rbc.com/activate

    Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox) and type rbc.com/activate directly into the address bar. Verify the HTTPS padlock appears before proceeding.

  2. 2

    Sign In or Enroll in RBC Online Banking

    Log in with your existing RBC Online Banking credentials. If you are a new RBC customer without an online banking profile, click "Enroll Now" and follow the prompts using your Social Insurance Number (SIN) and date of birth.

  3. 3

    Enter Your Card Details

    On the activation screen, enter your 16-digit card number, the expiry date from the front of the card, and the 3-digit CVV code from the back (next to the signature strip).

  4. 4

    Verify Your Identity

    RBC will ask you to confirm your registered mailing address or answer a security question. This step protects you if the card was intercepted before delivery.

  5. 5

    Click Activate — You're Done!

    Press the Activate button. A confirmation message will appear on screen and you may receive an email confirmation. Your card is active and ready to use immediately — online, in-store, and internationally.

💡 Pro Tip: After activation, consider setting or changing your PIN through RBC Online Banking under Manage Cards → Change PIN. This is required for chip-and-PIN in-store transactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about activating an RBC credit card.

How do I activate my RBC credit card at rbc.com/activate?
Go to rbc.com/activate, sign in to your RBC Online Banking account, enter your 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV when prompted, confirm your registered Canadian billing address, and click Activate. Your card becomes active instantly.
How long does activation at rbc.com/activate take?
Online activation at rbc.com/activate typically takes 2 to 5 minutes. Once you click Activate and see the confirmation message, your card is live and ready to use — there is no additional waiting period.
What is the RBC credit card activation phone number?
RBC provides a dedicated activation phone line available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. French service is also available. Find the official contact number on rbc.com.
Can I activate my RBC card without online banking?
Yes. RBC offers a 24/7 phone activation option — find the number on rbc.com. The automated system will ask for your card number and personal details. No online banking profile is needed. You can also visit your nearest RBC branch with photo ID.
Is rbc.com/activate safe and legitimate?
Yes. The portal is operated by Royal Bank of Canada and uses 256-bit TLS encryption plus multi-factor authentication. Always type the URL directly into your browser — never click on email or SMS links claiming to be RBC activation links, as these are common phishing attempts.
What if rbc.com/activate is not working?
Try these steps: (1) Clear your browser cache and cookies, (2) Try a different browser or incognito/private window, (3) Disable browser extensions temporarily, (4) Check if RBC Online Banking is under scheduled maintenance (usually 3–5 AM EST). If the issue persists, activate via the RBC Mobile app or contact RBC by phone (number available at rbc.com).
Can I activate a replacement or renewal RBC card the same way?
Yes. Replacement cards (issued for loss, theft, damage, or expiry) are activated through the exact same process at rbc.com/activate. Once your new card is activated, your previous card is automatically and permanently deactivated.
Which RBC credit cards can be activated at rbc.com/activate?
All RBC personal credit cards are supported, including: RBC Avion Visa Infinite, RBC Cashback Mastercard, RBC Rewards+ Visa, RBC ION Visa, RBC ION+ Visa, RBC British Airways Visa Infinite, RBC Westjet World Elite Mastercard, and all RBC prepaid cards.
Do I need to set a PIN separately after activation?
Your PIN is typically mailed separately or may already be set. After activation, you can change your PIN at any time through RBC Online Banking under My Accounts → Manage Cards → Change PIN, or at any RBC ABM using your existing PIN.
How do I activate my RBC card if I'm outside Canada?
Visit rbc.com/activate from any device outside Canada — it works internationally as long as you have your RBC Online Banking credentials. If you need phone support from abroad, visit rbc.com for international contact details.

Security Tips for RBC Card Activation in 2026

Protect yourself from fraud when activating your RBC credit card.

🔒

Always type rbc.com/activate into your browser. Never use links from emails or texts you did not request.

Enable 2FA

Turn on two-factor authentication in your RBC Online Banking settings to add an extra layer of security.

⏰

Activate within 30 days

Activate your card promptly after receiving it. Prolonged inactivity may trigger a security review by RBC.

🔔

Set up RBC Alerts

After activation, enable real-time transaction alerts through RBC Online Banking to catch unauthorized activity immediately.

📦

Shred your packaging

Shred any card packaging or paper inserts that display your partial card number or account details before recycling.
